CULM BRAND RULES

NAME AND ARTWORK

Write the brand as Culm and the project handle as culmjs. Use the supplied
outlines instead of retyping the logo in another font. The artwork is a custom
vector reconstruction of concept 08, with straightened edges that preserve
its pixel character.

COLOR PALETTE

Charcoal   #262626
Amber      #FF9400
Cream      #FFF9DF
White      #FFFFFF

Use the supplied charcoal-and-amber versions on light backgrounds. Use the
cream-and-amber reverse versions on dark backgrounds. All-black and all-white
versions are provided for monochrome applications. Keep the supplied color
assignments; do not recolor individual parts.

CLEAR SPACE

Leave at least one square accent width of empty space on every side of the
artwork. Use the amber square accent in the chosen logo as the measuring unit.
For a monochrome version, use that same square's width. For icon-only artwork,
use the accent width from its corresponding full lockup at the same mark size.

Measure from the outermost visible artwork. Keep text, borders, and other
graphics outside this area. The export canvas may already include padding;
check the placement rather than assuming the canvas edge supplies enough.

SUGGESTED MINIMUM DISPLAY SIZES

Widths refer to visible artwork, excluding any export canvas padding.

Icon                  16 px wide
Wordmark             120 px wide
Horizontal logo      160 px wide
Signature with handle 240 px wide

These are design starting points, not tested guarantees. Inspect the actual
placement at its final size. Increase the size if gaps close or the lettering
loses clarity. Judge stacked versions by their wordmark size. Omit the handle
when it cannot be read comfortably. Use the icon alone for small favicons and
profile pictures.

SHAPE AND PLACEMENT

Keep square corners, straight edges, and the original proportions. Do not
stretch, rotate, skew, round, or redraw the artwork. Do not rearrange the mark,
wordmark, or handle. Use another supplied layout when the space requires it.

Do not add outlines, shadows, gradients, textures, or effects. Place the logo
on a background where its full silhouette and internal gaps remain clear.
Avoid busy imagery directly behind it. Preserve any transparent background.

FORMAT AND PRODUCTION

Prefer SVG for websites and layouts that need scaling. Use PNG for uploads;
the manifest index records each export's exact pixel dimensions. Do not enlarge
a small PNG to create a print file.

Use PDF or EPS for print when requested by the supplier. Colors are defined in
RGB. Ask the printer to manage conversion for the intended paper, ink, and
process, then approve a physical or suitable production proof. No universal
CMYK or spot-color match is specified in this kit.

For embroidery, engraving, stamps, or other limited-detail processes, begin
with a supplied monochrome version. Ask the supplier to check minimum gaps
and strokes, and inspect a sample before producing the full run.

FUTURE REVISIONS

Keep the source artwork as the reference. Export new sizes from the vector
source, retain the same geometry, and record additions in the manifest index.
Treat a change to proportions, spacing, colors, or lettering as a new design
revision rather than a routine export.
